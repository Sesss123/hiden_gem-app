Delete Your Account

Hidden Gems SL account and data deletion

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Hidden Gems SL and sign in.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review the warning and confirm the deletion request.

Request deletion without the app

Email pathiranasehas3@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Use the subject Hidden Gems SL Account Deletion Request.

For security, we may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days and will confirm when the request has been completed.

Data that is deleted

We delete the account profile and associated personal data controlled by Hidden Gems SL, including authentication identifiers, saved preferences, wishlists, uploaded profile content, family-sharing links, device notification tokens, and account-linked records that are not legally required to be retained.

Limited records may be retained only where required for security, fraud prevention, legal, tax, or transaction-record obligations. Retained data is not used for unrelated purposes.

Subscriptions

Deleting your Hidden Gems SL account does not automatically cancel a subscription managed by Google Play. Cancel any active subscription first from Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions to prevent future renewal charges.
