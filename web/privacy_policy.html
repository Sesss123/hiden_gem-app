Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026

Introduction

Hidden Gems SL ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we use it, when it is shared, how long it is retained, and the choices available to you when you use our mobile application and related services (the "App"). If you have questions or concerns, contact us at pathiranasehas3@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

  • Account and Contact Data: Display name, email address, phone number and verification status, Firebase user ID, profile picture, preferred language, role, and account settings.
  • Location and Family Sharing Data: With your permission, we process approximate or precise location while the App is in use for discovery, navigation, safety, tour-session tracking, meeting points, and travel calculations. If you enable Family Sharing, we also process recipient names or labels, temporary share tokens, selected trip status, guide identity, emergency status, and live coordinates made available to people you authorize. The Android App does not request background location permission.
  • Guide and Marketplace Data: Guide biographies, languages, regions, availability, pricing, license information, identity documents, selfies, vehicle details, listing and vehicle photos, bank or mobile-wallet payout details, and verification decisions.
  • Bookings, Communications, and User Content: Booking requests, itineraries, tour packages, session records, broadcasts, traveler-guide communications, reviews, ratings, reports, block records, saved places, and other content you submit. Public profiles, approved listings, photos, ratings, and reviews may be visible to other signed-in users.
  • Camera, Photos, Files, and Microphone: Camera access supports QR scanning, food scanning, profile/listing photos, and identity documents. Microphone access supports voice-assistant and speech-to-text features. Camera frames, selected images, audio-derived text, or uploaded files are processed only when you start the related feature. Uploaded content is retained as described below.
  • AI Feature Data: Prompts, trip preferences, itinerary context, selected or captured food images, voice-derived text, language choices, generated responses, and related safety/moderation reports may be sent through our authenticated API to our self-hosted AI infrastructure to provide chat, itinerary, recommendation, translation, and food-analysis features.
  • Payments and Purchases: Subscription product, purchase history, entitlement and renewal status, booking payment status, transaction identifiers, payout requests, and refund or cancellation information. We do not store complete payment-card numbers; payment credentials are handled by the applicable payment provider.
  • App, Analytics, and Device Data: App interactions, searches, feature usage, diagnostics, performance information, crash logs, IP/network information, Firebase installation and messaging tokens, device/app identifiers, advertising ID, App Check/integrity signals, security events, and fraud or location-spoofing indicators.
  • Calendar and Notification Data: With your permission, the App can add or manage travel plans in your device calendar and use an FCM push token to deliver booking, safety, account, and service notifications.

How We Use Your Information

Having accurate information about you permits us to provide you with a smooth, efficient, and customized experience. Specifically, we may use information collected about you via the App to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide location-based recommendations, navigation, bookings, tour coordination, Family Sharing, emergency and safety features.
  • Review Guide applications, publish approved marketplace content, process reports, enforce blocks, and moderate user-generated content.
  • Process AI prompts and media to produce requested itineraries, translations, recommendations, chat responses, and food-analysis results.
  • Monitor and analyze usage and trends to improve your experience using Firebase Analytics.
  • Provide subscriptions, restore purchases, process guide bookings and payouts, display advertising, and measure ad performance.
  • Deliver notifications and customer support.
  • Prevent fraud, spam, abuse, account compromise, payment manipulation, and location spoofing; investigate incidents and comply with legal obligations.

When Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal data. Information is shared only as needed to provide the service, at your direction, or for security and legal reasons:

  • Other Users: Public Guide profiles, approved listings, reviews, ratings, and selected photos are shown to signed-in users. Booking and tour-session participants receive the information required to coordinate that service.
  • People You Authorize: Family Sharing recipients can view only the trip, identity, location, meeting-point, and emergency fields you enable until the link expires or is revoked.
  • Moderators and Administrators: Authorized personnel may review Guide documents, reports, disputed content, safety incidents, and fraud/security records.
  • Service Providers: Providers listed below process information for hosting, authentication, analytics, payments, advertising, maps, weather, notifications, and AI operations under their own terms and our service arrangements.
  • Legal and Safety: We may preserve or disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users, investigate fraud or abuse, or respond to a valid legal request.

Third-Party Service Providers

We may share your information with third-party vendors, service providers, contractors, or agents who perform services for us or on our behalf. This includes:

  • Google Firebase: Used for authentication, Firestore/database and storage, Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging, App Check, and related security operations.
  • Google Maps and OpenWeather: Used for maps, navigation context, and weather information.
  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertising, manage consent, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance.
  • RevenueCat and Google Play Billing: Used to process subscriptions, validate purchases, and restore purchases.
  • PayHere: Used to process payments for real-world Guide bookings and return payment status to the App.
  • Hidden Gems SL API and AI Infrastructure: Our Laravel API and self-hosted AI services process authenticated requests, user reports, bookings, security signals, prompts, and media needed for App features.

International Data Transfers

Our service providers and cloud infrastructure may process information outside Sri Lanka. Where data is transferred across borders, we use reasonable contractual, technical, and organizational safeguards and limit processing to the purposes described in this policy.

Data Retention

  • Family Sharing links and associated access expire after the duration you select or when you revoke them.
  • Account, profile, booking, content, and subscription records are retained while your account is active and as needed to provide the service.
  • AI inputs and outputs, analytics, diagnostics, and support records are retained only for operational, safety, improvement, or legal needs and are deleted or de-identified when no longer required.
  • After account deletion, deletion begins immediately. Limited transaction, fraud, security, moderation, tax, or legal records may be retained or anonymized for the period required by law or legitimate safety obligations. Backup copies expire through normal backup rotation.

Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information. While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the personal information you provide to us (such as encrypting data transmissions via HTTPS), please be aware that despite our efforts, no security measures are perfect or impenetrable.

Your Rights

You can review or change account information in the App, disable optional device permissions in system settings, revoke Family Sharing links, change advertising-consent choices from the App's privacy options, report content, and block or unblock users. You can permanently request account and associated-data deletion from Profile > Delete Account, or through our account deletion page. You may also contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or information about your personal data, subject to applicable law.

Children's Privacy

The App is designed for adult travelers and tourism professionals and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly solicit personal data from minors. If we learn that a minor has provided personal data without appropriate authorization, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to remove it.
