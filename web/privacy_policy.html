Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 07, 2026

Introduction

Hidden Gems SL ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). If you have any questions or concerns about this policy, please contact us at support@hiddengems.lk.

Information We Collect

  • Personal Data: When you register for an account or apply to be a Guide, we may collect personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, profile picture, and identity verification documents (e.g., National Identity Card, Driving License).
  • Location Data (Foreground and Background): To provide core features of the App, such as real-time navigation to Hidden Gems, displaying nearby events, and location-based AR experiences, we request access to your device's location services. We collect precise and approximate location data when the app is running in the foreground. For Tour Guides, we may collect background location data during an active tour session to ensure the safety and tracking of the journey.
  • Device Camera and Microphone: The App requests access to your device's Camera and Microphone to provide Augmented Reality (AR) experiences (via ARCore) and Real-Time Food Scanning features. The camera feed is processed in real-time. We do not record or store your camera feed on our servers unless you explicitly take a photo and choose to upload it.

How We Use Your Information

Having accurate information about you permits us to provide you with a smooth, efficient, and customized experience. Specifically, we may use information collected about you via the App to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide real-time location-based recommendations and navigation.
  • Enable AR (Augmented Reality) features to display historical overlays on your camera screen.
  • Monitor and analyze usage and trends to improve your experience using Firebase Analytics.
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions, monitor against location spoofing, and protect against criminal activity.

Third-Party Service Providers

We may share your information with third-party vendors, service providers, contractors, or agents who perform services for us or on our behalf. This includes:

  • Google Firebase: Used for authentication, cloud storage, database management, and crash analytics.
  • Google Maps & ARCore: Used for map rendering and Augmented Reality features.

Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information. While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the personal information you provide to us (such as encrypting data transmissions via HTTPS), please be aware that despite our efforts, no security measures are perfect or impenetrable.

Your Rights

You can review or change the information in your account or terminate your account at any time by logging into the App's settings or contacting us directly. Upon your request to terminate your account, we will deactivate or delete your account and information from our active databases.
